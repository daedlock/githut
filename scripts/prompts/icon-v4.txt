THE GLYPH — ROUNDED GEOMETRIC HUT ON TRANSPARENT CANVAS
A single geometric hut silhouette, isolated on a transparent 1024x1024 canvas. The hut occupies about 70% of the canvas width, vertically centered, with approximately 15% transparent padding on all sides.

The hut is a pentagonal shape with SOFTLY ROUNDED edges on every corner and every transition:
- Symmetrical triangular pitched roof (roof angle approximately 50 degrees from horizontal)
- Two vertical walls
- A flat base
- Base corners rounded with approximately 28px radius
- Roof-to-wall seams rounded with approximately 24px radius
- Roof peak rounded into a smooth apex (not a sharp point)
- Wall height equals roughly half the roof height

The silhouette reads as pillowy, friendly, tactile — like a rounded iOS app glyph.

The hut is filled with a rich warm multi-stop gradient:
- Base color: warm burnt orange (#ea580c)
- Upper-right: warm amber (#fdba74)
- Bottom-left and along the shadowed left wall: deeper orange-red (#c2410c)

This creates dimensional lighting — the hut feels illuminated from the upper-right even though the form stays flat and vector-clean. No chimney, no door, no wall texture.
